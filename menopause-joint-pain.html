The problem

"It's just your age" is not an answer.

You wake up stiff. Your hands, knees, hips or shoulders ache. Your X-rays look fine, and you were told to take a pain pill and wait. More than 7 in 10 women have joint or muscle symptoms around menopause.1 You are not imagining it.

Dr. Status Quo

"That's just aging."

  • Says your X-rays look fine, so nothing can be done
  • Offers pain pills and "wait and see"
  • Treats each sore joint by itself
  • Never asks what changed in your body
Dr. Root Cause

Finds out why, then fixes it.

  • Checks how you move, not just the picture
  • Looks at joints, muscle, inflammation and nutrition together
  • Builds one plan from your own results

Measure first

Three ways we find the cause.

We don't guess. These are the main tools we use to see why your joints hurt. Each one answers a different question.

01

Joint & Movement Exam

A hands-on exam of the joints that hurt, plus a look at how your whole body moves.

What it shows →

What it shows

  • Which joints are stiff, weak or overworked
  • How you squat, reach, walk and balance
  • Where the pain starts, not just where you feel it
02

Symptom Score (MSQ)

A short questionnaire that turns how you feel into a number we can track.

What it shows →

What it shows

  • Pain, sleep, energy, mood and digestion in one score
  • Which body systems carry the most load right now
  • Your score over time, so progress is clear
03

Targeted Lab Work

Blood tests chosen for you, used only when they will change your plan.

What it shows →

What it can show

  • Signs of inflammation
  • Vitamin D, blood sugar and thyroid, when needed
  • A starting point we can compare against later

These tests guide your plan. Which ones fit you is decided with Dr. Bekkum. We do not prescribe hormone therapy. If you use it or are thinking about it, we work alongside your medical provider.
